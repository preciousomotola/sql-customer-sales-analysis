# Customer & Sales Analysis — SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer and sales data using SQL to uncover business insights across customers, products, regions, and monthly sales.

The analysis focuses on:

- Customer performance
- Customer segmentation
- Product performance
- Regional revenue
- Monthly sales trends
- Revenue growth opportunities

## Business Questions

The analysis answers questions such as:

- Which customers generate the most revenue?
- Which customers are high-value?
- Which products perform best?
- Which regions generate the most revenue?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Is revenue growth driven by more orders or higher order values?

## Tools Used

- SQL
- SQLite
- Python
- Pandas
- Google Colab

## Key Findings

- Total revenue: **₦1.04 million**
- Total orders: **15**
- Rice was the top-performing product, generating **₦485,000**.
- Ada generated the highest total revenue at **₦295,000**.
- Emeka had the highest average order value at **₦95,000**.
- Ibadan generated the highest regional revenue at **₦580,000**.
- Monthly revenue increased from **₦290,000 in January** to **₦395,000 in March**.
- Monthly orders remained constant at 5, meaning revenue growth was driven mainly by higher-value purchases.

## Business Recommendations

1. Retain high-value customers through personalized offers and loyalty strategies.
2. Encourage medium- and low-value customers to increase purchase frequency and order value.
3. Maintain Rice's strong performance while exploring opportunities to improve Beans and Oil sales.
4. Continue strategies that encourage larger purchases.
5. Monitor regional performance and customer acquisition before expanding into new markets.

## Limitations

The dataset contains only 15 orders and 5 customers and covers January to March 2026. Therefore, the findings should be treated as exploratory rather than representative of long-term business performance.

The dataset also does not contain product cost information, so profitability could not be evaluated.
